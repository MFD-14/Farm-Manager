Privacy policy

Woodlands Farm Manager · operated by Musara Holdings LLC

Woodlands Farm Manager is a private tool used to run Woodlands Farm near Kwekwe, Zimbabwe. It is used only by the farm's owner and the people the owner gives an account to (such as the farm caretaker). It is not offered to the public.

What we collect

Account details (name, email address, optional phone number); records of farm work (tasks, readings, notes); and evidence photos, voice notes and the time and device they were captured on.

WhatsApp

If a person's phone number is added to their account and WhatsApp messages are switched on, the farm sends them task reminders, a morning task list, notices when a task is sent back, or (for owners and task managers) alerts, through the WhatsApp Business Platform operated by Meta. Replies, button taps and photos they send to the farm number are recorded against their farm tasks. Messages from numbers that don't belong to an account are ignored. Anyone can switch WhatsApp messages off in the app under My account, or by asking the farm owner.

Evidence photos, video and voice notes

Evidence is stored on the farm's own server with the task it belongs to, and is reviewed by the farm owner or a task manager, who accepts the task as complete or sends it back. It is not sent to any automated photo-checking service.

Who can see the data

The farm's owners and, for their own tasks, the people doing the work. Data is stored on the farm's own server. It is not sold or shared for advertising.

Keeping and deleting data

Farm records are kept as the farm's working history. To ask for your personal details to be removed, or for any privacy question, contact the farm owner (Musara Holdings LLC).
