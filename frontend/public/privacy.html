Privacy policy

Woodlands Farm Manager · operated by Musara Holdings LLC

Woodlands Farm Manager is a private tool used to run Woodlands Farm near Kwekwe, Zimbabwe. It is used only by the farm's owner and the people the owner gives an account to (such as the farm caretaker). It is not offered to the public.

What we collect

Account details (name, email address, optional phone number); records of farm work (tasks, readings, notes); and evidence photos, voice notes and the time and device they were captured on.

WhatsApp

If a person's phone number is added to their account and WhatsApp messages are switched on, the farm sends them task reminders, a morning task list, photo retake requests, or (for owners) alerts, through the WhatsApp Business Platform operated by Meta. Replies, button taps and photos they send to the farm number are recorded against their farm tasks. Messages from numbers that don't belong to an account are ignored. Anyone can switch WhatsApp messages off in the app under My account, or by asking the farm owner.

Photo checking

If the owner switches it on, evidence photos are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to check that they show the task was done. Anthropic processes them to return a verdict and does not use API data to train its models by default.

Who can see the data

The farm's owners and, for their own tasks, the people doing the work. Data is stored on the farm's own server. It is not sold or shared for advertising.

Keeping and deleting data

Farm records are kept as the farm's working history. To ask for your personal details to be removed, or for any privacy question, contact the farm owner (Musara Holdings LLC).
